# Code of Conduct

This repository is a scientific publication record. All interactions —
issues, comments, and discussions — must meet basic standards of
professional and scholarly conduct.

---

## Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Scientific accuracy and intellectual honesty
- Respectful engagement with authors and other contributors
- Clear attribution of claims and sources
- Acknowledgment of scope limitations and uncertainty

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Misrepresentation of the paper's findings
- Fabricating results or claiming false reproductions
- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Spam, commercial solicitation, or off-topic promotion

---

## Enforcement

Issues and comments that violate this code will be closed and removed.
Repeated violations may result in reporting to GitHub.

The maintainers' decisions on enforcement are final.
